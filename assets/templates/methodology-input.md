# Optional methodology figure brief

Use this as a starting prompt, not a required form. Existing context may answer these points.

Use $daxuan-science-assets to create a transparent, editable methodology SVG and PNG.

- **Purpose and type:** What should readers understand? Principle, process, or both?
- **Inputs:** Paper files/links/DOIs and relevant methods, legends, or supplements; or my preliminary idea below.
- **My idea or adaptation:** What I propose, what comes from the papers, and what changes.
- **Scope:** Where the diagram starts and ends; required nodes, branches, controls, relationships, and exclusions.
- **Content budget:** Intended main figure/supplement/slides, physical width, panel count, level of detail, and any label limit.
- **Placement, if applicable:** Existing figure screenshot, intended empty region, heading already present, and full figure width if known. Estimate missing geometry from the reference and state any print-size assumption.
- **Reference-author style:** Relevant reference-paper figures or graphical abstract; what to learn from their color hierarchy.
- **Target-figure style:** Current figure/slot, neighboring modules, manuscript palette/report, and any fixed element-to-color meanings.
- **Visual preferences:** Label language and scale relative to icons, approved colors to retain, and components that need independent editing.
- **Scientific constraints:** Required order/parameters, source disagreements, assumptions, and unresolved questions.

Extract the supported method before drawing. Keep source-supported steps, my specifications, adaptations, and proposals distinguishable in the evidence sidecar. Do not invent results or citations. Use native SVG for labels and relations; image exploration may help complex illustration components. Show the actual transparent PNG and provide the editable SVG.
